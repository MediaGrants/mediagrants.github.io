How this works. An automated sweep re-reads the websites of the funders and aggregators listed in data/sources.json and rewrites data/grants.json. Everything on this page comes from that file.

Always check the funder's own page before you apply. Deadlines move, calls are withdrawn, and eligibility rules change between rounds. Entries carry the date they were last verified; treat anything older than two weeks as a lead, not a fact.

Know a funder we are missing? Local and national funders are exactly what an automated sweep cannot find on its own — a journalist in the country usually knows them first. Suggest one here — it takes a minute and needs only a free GitHub account.
