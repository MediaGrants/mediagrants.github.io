We invest in businesses that keep society informed and democracy alive

30+ years. The only global nonprofit investor dedicated to independent media and information

Who we are

We are the only global non-profit impact investment fund financing businesses that keep society informed and democracy alive. Through investment and strategic support, we build the commercial strength these businesses need to stay independent and better serve the public. When they grow stronger, so does society.

We have done this since 1995, in places where access to independent information is under threat and democracy is under strain. We operate in the space between philanthropy and commercial investment, backed by blended capital from foundations, development institutions and private investors.

Grow your business with MDIF

Investment that supports growth and independence

We are the only global non-profit impact investment fund financing businesses that keep society informed and democracy alive. We have done this since 1995, providing loans and equity to 162 independent businesses in 47 countries.

Why partner with MDIF?

Financing aligned with your business and mission – You get a loan or equity investment tailored to your business needs, whether you re looking to expand an established company, grow an emerging business or protect your independence when it s at risk. Our financing is designed to support your long-term success rather than prioritize short-term profits. We never seek influence over your content. Is my company eligible?
We welcome applications from independent businesses with a clear public-interest mission and a commitment to long-term financial sustainability. All applicants must meet specific requirements, so please review our full eligibility criteria to see whether your company qualifies.
The applicant must work in a country (i) with a history of media oppression or ownership concentration, (ii) that is in a state of democratic transition, and/or (iii) suffers from information poverty. MDIF only provides financing in countries that have been approved by our Board of Directors. On receiving an application from a news outlet in a country in which we have not worked, the Board will decide whether it is a country we can work in after analysing its media, legal and business environments. Only once that has been confirmed will we consider the merits of an application.
The applicant must have a capable and responsible management and must apply for funding with a detailed business plan. The plan must aim to broaden and strengthen the applicant’s operation, must have strong prospects that the loans will be repaid and/or invested capital recovered, and should have the goal of turning the applying organization into, if not a profitable, then at least a self-sustaining entity. The applicant must also agree to provide MDIF with unrestricted access to its accounting records.
Application process
Before applying, we encourage you to review our full application process. It involves several stages, from assessing your mission and business plan to due diligence and negotiating financing terms. We evaluate applications against three core criteria: mission impact, long-term financial viability and organizational integrity.
Application process
Once an applicant has filled out an inquiry form and we have checked that they meet our basic eligibility criteria, including that the country is one we are able to work in, the next step is securing programmatic approval from our Board of Directors.

Our funds

Investing in reliable information

We invest in independent media and information businesses through debt, equity, hybrid and venture funds, each shaped by what those businesses need and what our investors want to achieve.

Funds marked “currently open to investors” are taking new investment. If you’re a donor or foundation, there are other ways to support our work.

See how to invest or fund our work

MDIF General Fund

MDIF s original evergreen fund provides debt and equity financing to businesses worldwide. Since 1996, it has invested more than $100 million in businesses across 40 countries.

MDIF Western Balkans Fund
MDIF Western Balkans Fund invests across Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Through investment and strategic support, it backs innovative startups, growing media businesses undergoing digital transformation and outlets at risk of capture.
Our closed funds include the Emerging Media Opportunity Fund (EMOF), a private equity fund linking MDIF’s full carry to social impact; MDIF Media Finance (MMF), a blended loan fund backed by a 55% first-loss guarantee from the Swedish International Development Cooperation Agency (Sida).
Other past initiatives included listing the first impact bond for press freedom on the Zurich Stock Exchange and launching Free Press Investment Notes, which allowed US investors to invest in MDIF from as little as $1,000.

Our funders and impact investors

Blended capital for independent information

Our blended capital model brings together philanthropic funding and private investment. Philanthropic funding plays a catalytic role by reducing investment risks and covering the costs associated with financing and strategic support. Investors bring in capital to create positive social impact while earning a financial return. The diversity of our funding base helps protect our independence and that of the businesses we support.

A selection of our funders and investors

Our work is backed by a diverse group of foundations, development institutions, impact investors and individuals united by a shared commitment to an informed society and stronger democracy.
