About the Impact Fellowships Network (IFN)
The Impact Fellowships Network (IFN) is a multi-year initiative, with core funding from the W.K. Kellogg Foundation, designed to elevate the global fellowship sector and strengthen its ability to develop leaders who drive meaningful change. Building on the success of the Im

--- relevant lines ---
pact Fellowships Summit (IFS), the Fellowship Alumni Network Study (FANS) and the Impact Fellowships Network (IFN) 2026 Impact Study, IFN is building a dynamic, inclusive community of practice focused on leadership development and social impact.
IFN brings together over 850 fellowship leaders, designers, and practitioners from across sectors and regions—united by a shared belief that bringing people together to bridge divides, investing in opportunities for them to learn and grow, and supporting their development as leaders can change the world.
IFN offers a dynamic, collaborative space where members strengthen their programs to support leaders tackling unprecedented challenges, share what works and what doesn’t, and elevate the collective impact of fellowships. Through continuous learning and adaptation, we help ensure IFN members are not only resilient but also equipped to lead transformative change in their communities and sectors—advancing our shared mission in a rapidly changing world.
"IFN has empowered us at Library Aid Africa to enhance our approaches to supporting alumni of the Young African Library Leaders Fellowship as they integrate into library ecosystems in their countries and contribute to meaningful change within their institutions, communities, and systems" - Damilare Oyedele, Library Aid Africa
Our Role in the Fellowship Ecosystem
The Impact Fellowships Network (IFN) is a key node in the global fellowship ecosystem—accelerating innovation and providing a trusted space for fellowship practitioners to learn, share, and grow.
At the heart of our mission is a commitment to strengthening the fellowship ecosystem through:
Fellowship Impact Research - Collaboration fuels IFN’s approach to learning and action. Through co-designed research and training, we support partners in evaluating program impact in ways that are responsive, relevant and replicable – building shared knowledge across our network.
Join the Impact Fellowship Network
Become an IFN Member for year-round access to resources, peer collaboration, and a growing movement shaping the future of fellowships.
