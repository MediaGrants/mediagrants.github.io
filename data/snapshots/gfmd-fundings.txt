Organisation: MacArthur Foundation

Status: Open
Type: Grant

Theme: Media support

Funding Size: USD $500,000 to $1 million
Deadline: 09/02/2027

Eligibility: Community-led USA or USA territories
Target Region: USA
Application language: English
Together in Purpose is a USD $26 million open call celebrates locally rooted, community-led collaborations that bring people together around a shared purpose and help communities remain connected, participatory, resilient, and vibrant.
Together in Purpose will award up to 40 flexible grants ranging from USD $500,000 to $1 million for a three-year term. In addition to providing funding, Together in Purpose aims to elevate practical insights about how enduring relationships, community leadership, and sustained collaboration help communities navigate challenges,

--- relevant lines ---
 remain connected, and move forward together. Organizations headquartered in and conducting their work within the United States or U.S. Territories are encouraged to assess their eligibility and fit.
Eligibility
Applicants should demonstrate at least three years of collaboration (as of the Together in Purpose registration deadline January 14, 2027), with other organizations or groups.
This collaboration should be for work that creates consistent opportunities for collective actions that strengthen relationships within the communities served. In the case of collaborations, one eligible Lead Organization and one legal entity must submit the Application and serve as the potential grant recipient.
Applicants must also have the organizational capacity and financial and operational systems needed to responsibly manage and administer grant funding.
Interested organizations must register by January 14, 2027, at 5:00 pm U.S. Central Time , to submit an application. Complete applications must be submitted by February 9, 2027, at 5:00 pm U.S. Central Time.
How to apply
After registering, Applicants will receive a confirmation email and instructions for accessing the Application. Apply here .

Organisation: European Commission

Status: Open
Type: Grant

Theme: Media Innovation

Funding Size: maximum EUR 2 million
Deadline: 04/02/2027

Eligibility: European news media
Target Region: Europe
Application language: English
This 7th annual call supports cross-border, collaborative proposals between news media organisations focusing on innovative business models and/or journalistic projects across Europe. It aims to help European news media become more sustainable and resilient by supporting cross-border media collaborations. Projects are expected to last 24 months.
Eligibility
Consortia may include non-profit, public and private media outlets (including written/online press, radio/podcasts, TV) as well as other organisations focusing on news media (including m

--- relevant lines ---
edia associations, NGOs, journalistic funds and training organisations focusing on media professionals).
Natural persons are not eligible (with the exception of self-employed persons, i.e. sole traders, where the company does not have legal personality separate from that of the natural person).
Since the opening intends to achieve sector-wide impacts, proposals are invited to involve as many news media as possible in their activities.
The maximum co-financing rate is 80% of the total eligible costs, so applicants need to co-finance their projects.
Proposals should include activities that best contribute to the consortium’s goals, such as events, online trainings and workshops, exchange programmes, mentoring schemes, mapping of best practices, development of technical standards, guidelines and editorial standards, practical guidebooks, development and testing of platforms and technical solutions, and promotional activities.
Financial support to third parties is allowed for grants and prizes under the conditions set in the call.
How to Apply
Submit your proposal directly online via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal Electronic Submission System before 04/02/2027. Paper applications are not accepted. Proposals will be evaluated in spring, and results communicated in summer 2027. Apply here .

Organisation: European Commission

Status: Open
Type: Grant

Theme: Media support

Funding Size: Maximum EUR 2.5 million
Deadline: 04/02/2027

Eligibility: European intermediary organisations that set up and distribute funds
Target Region: Europe
Application language: English
This call supports intermediary organisations that set up and distribute funds to news media in sectors of particular relevance to democracy, such as local and regional media, community media, investigative journalism, and public interest news. Projects are expected to start towards the end of 2027 and last 24 months.
Eligibility
The call relies on intermediary organisations active in the media domain. Financial support will be awarded to these organisations so that they can set up financing scheme

--- relevant lines ---
The call is open to entities such as media associations, NGOs, non-profit organisations, civil society organisations, public authorities, international organisations, universities, research centres, journalistic funds and training organisations focusing on media professionals, profitmaking entities, foundations, etc.
Proposals may be submitted either by single applicants or by a consortium.
The funding schemes must focus on the European Union, and specifically on areas with low provision of the specific news described above, or in media markets where media pluralism is strained.
Funding schemes should target news media sectors of special relevance to democracy and civic participation, characterised by their role in enhancing democracy, shaping the public debate and bringing benefits to their audiences and communities, instead of focusing just on profit. These sectors include in particular local and regional media; independent and investigative journalism; and organisations delivering public interest news/public service journalism (such as community, legal and civic journalism and media, news increasing transparency about the media).
Setting up a funding scheme for media organisations is compulsory.
The maximum co-financing rate is 90% of the total eligible costs, so applicants need to co-finance their projects (but their grantees should not be required to co-finance).
The funds can focus on activities that contribute to transforming the work of targeted parties, for instance innovations in editorial production, news coverage and revenue models; improvement of distribution and dissemination of news; audience development and engagement and community-building strategies; development of technical tools; and training on these topics.
Applicants can also propose accompanying activities to the fund (development of standards, indicators, repositories of knowledge, legal advice, trainings, etc).
How to Apply
Submit your proposal directly online via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal Electronic Submission System before 04/02/2027. Paper applications are not accepted. Proposals will be evaluated in spring, and results communicated in summer 2027. Apply here .

Organisation: International Women s Media Foundation

Status: Open
Type: Grant

Theme: Freedom of expression

Funding Size: USD 2,500–USD 10,000
Deadline: Ongoing

Eligibility: USA journalists writing about discrimination
Target Region: USA
Application Language: English
The IWMF s Fault Lines Reporting Fund supports journalists reporting on the forces driving racism, antisemitism, Islamophobia, misogyny, and other forms of intolerance promoting hate in the United States. Successful applications examine how false, divisive, and harmful narratives — such as stories that promote or normalise hate, discrimination, and the dehumanisation of individuals and communities — take hold, including what is fuelling them, where they are most pronounced, the scope of their impact, and where individuals and communities

--- relevant lines ---
 are making real progress in combating these hate-based agendas. The three-year programme provides grants to staff and freelance journalists planning well-researched, investigative, and evidence-based reporting within the United States. Grant funds may be used to cover travel, logistics, security, insurance, and other reporting-related expenses.
Eligibility
The applicant s primary profession must be journalism; applications will be accepted from freelance, staff, or content-based journalists.
A completed application in English (reporting itself may be conducted in any language).
A well-developed story pitch demonstrating investigative merit, originality, reach, potential impact, and need for funding.
Grantees must publish in a U.S. outlet within one year of receiving a grant and must credit the IWMF.
How to Apply
Submit the application through the IWMF Submittable portal on a rolling basis; there is no fixed closing date for the current programme cycle. Larger awards are considered on a case-by-case basis. Apply here .
