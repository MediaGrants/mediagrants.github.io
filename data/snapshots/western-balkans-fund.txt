About the Programme

People-to-people links at the heart of regional cooperation

MOVE Grants is a dedicated grant-making mechanism designed to support mobility, exchange, and people-to-people cooperation across the region. The scheme is built on WBF's belief that direct, in-person interactions whether through academic exchange, artistic collaboration, and/or professional mobility are vital to building trust, understanding, and shared 

--- relevant lines ---
Each MOVE grantee commits to an agreed structured mobility activity with a formal host institution/organisation in another Contracting Party of the Western Balkans Fund. After the visit, they share their experience within their networks, on social media and through WBF's platforms, becoming ambassadors for regional cooperation.
The programme funds short-term, individual mobility within the WB6: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Full eligibility rules, grant amounts and the submission deadline for Call 7 are set out in the Guidelines for Applicants and in the call package on OGMS.

Browse the full archive of MOVE projects on the Our Grantees page.
Before you apply
MOVE funds an individual travelling to a host institution in another Contracting Party. A few structural rules decide whether an application is eligible at all, and they are checked before anything else is read.
A MOVE application has to rest on a formal partnership with a host institution in another Contracting Party . The partnership is what confirms your mobility is grounded in a structured, mutually agreed framework rather than a self-organised trip.
You prove it with an official invitation letter from the host institution , stating the dates, the programme and the reason you are being invited. A confirmed registration, acceptance or enrolment letter for a course, programme or workshop they organise works too. Without this document the application is rejected at the eligibility check.
Activities should take place in the region. In exceptional, justified cases activities in the European Union are eligible where they promote the visibility of the Western Balkans internationally.

Grants do not exceed €10,000 and cover up to half of a project's total eligible costs , with the remaining share required to come from another donor rather than the applicant's own resources. The WB6-V4 Fellowships allow WB6 citizens to apply for short-term fellowships at V4 research institutions (in Czechia, Poland, Hungary, or Slovakia). Reciprocally, V4 applicants can apply for fellowships at WB6 institutions (in Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Kosovo* or Serbia).
The programme is jointly funded by the International Visegrad Fund and the Western Balkans Fund , reflecting a shared commitment to academic cooperation and people-to-people links between the two regions.
Fellowships run for a limited period of 2 to 10 weeks , during which the fellow conducts or participates in research, holds lectures or seminars at higher-education level, or studies sets of written or other materials in public archives or libraries.
The type of host institution is not prescribed but must be relevant to the nature of the fellowship — generally private or public higher-education institutions (colleges, universities, science academies), research bodies, libraries and archives, or laboratories.
Two directions, one fellowship
