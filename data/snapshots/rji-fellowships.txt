RJI Professional Innovation Fellowships

The Reynolds Journal

--- relevant lines ---
ism Institute invites proposals from individuals and organizations who wish to build practical and innovative resources for community-centered news, journalists and the communities they serve.
The 2025 RJI Fellows present their resources, tools and platforms built during the Fellowship! All these resources are free for any journalist or newsroom to utilize.
Applications for the 2027 RJI Professional Innovation Fellows will open November 2, 2026.
Fellowship Q&A Webinars:
October 14 @ 11am CT: Register to join us
November 10 @ 1pm CT: Register to join us
All fellowship projects are built, tested and launched to the public within the span of the fellowship and must be free, accessible and open-source for journalists, newsrooms and the public to utilize.
Our 2026 RJI Professional Innovation Fellows are building:
Whatever your idea, it will be built, tested and launched to be utilized by other news organizations, journalists and the people who depend on them by the end of your fellowship. You will publish articles , build fundraising strategy, create social logo & branding standards, host webinars and other public facing deliverables to share what you re learning for newsrooms and journalists, prepare for launch and ensure the long-term sustainability of your tool. When launched your tool will need to be immediately available to any journalist in the US to utilize (global is great too!) and free to use. Tools/resources/platforms focused on only one state or city are unlikely to be funded.
The final deliverable of your fellowship will be a publicly available resource that journalists and newsrooms can utilize whether it s a tool, platform, or workshop .
Types of RJI Professional Innovation Fellowships:
Institutional Fellowship: A RJI Fellow embedded at an organization to build out a specific resource, tool, program or product that addresses a current need, gap or challenge for that organization but is also applicable to other journalists and organizations in the industry.
This Fellowship is for individuals who want to work within an organization’s environment to collaborate across teams and benefit from the support and partnership of the organization. Student Innovation Fellowships

How do I apply?

Apply here.


--- relevant lines ---
Students: Applications close November 8, 2026. Students will be notified by email about fellowship interviews by November 13, 2026. Interviews will be conducted before winter break.
Newsrooms/Orgs: Applications close November 8, 2026. Potential mentors will be contacted by RJI to follow up on the application and discuss project details prior to being matched with a student.
What is a 2027 RJI Student Innovation Fellowship?
A Mizzou journalism student is placed with a news organization to work 30-40 hours per week in summer 2027. These fellowships are sponsored by RJI’s Palmer Innovation Endowment fund and the stipends are paid directly to the students.
These Fellowships are focused on projects that create, extend or build upon innovative work in each newsroom and transparently share that work with the industry by publishing articles through the Innovation in Focus series to help journalists learn from and emulate the innovative work.
Read articles from the 2026 RJI Student Innovation Fellows .
Each student fellow receives a minimum stipend of $8000 and will be expected to work for 30–40 hours a week for 12 weeks, starting mid-May and ending mid-August. Organizations are allowed (and encouraged!) to supplement this stipend with hourly pay.
Payments from RJI are made in three parts directly to the student. $1000 is paid prior to the Fellowship start to help with moving costs, then half the remaining stipend is paid after the beginning of the fellowship (within 30 days after the fellowship start) and the second half is paid after all the student s RJI deliverables are met.
These fellowships are designed for each student to work with a newsroom or journalism organization on innovative projects of mutual interest. Partner organizations are chosen based on their application answers, proposed projects and the availability of a student fellow whose interests match the skillsets needed for those projects.
Genevieve Smith, right, tabling with The Keene Sentinel during her RJI Student Innovation Fellowship.
