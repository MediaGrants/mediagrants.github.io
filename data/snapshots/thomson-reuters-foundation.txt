Our Work

Our Work
We work to strengthen information integrity, protect civil society and advance responsible business practices, through supporting independent media, access to law, and fair work & responsible AI.

Resilience of Independent Media Bolstering independent media that informs the public and upholds democracy.
Access to Law Enabling a fair legal system that is accessible to all to strengthen civic space.
Responsible Business Supporting businesses to thrive whilst doing no harm.

Our Services

Our Services
Through free legal assistance, data platforms, news, media development and events, our unique services foster free, fair and informed societies.

Media Development Services Providing capacity-building support to journalists and newsrooms.
TrustLaw The world’s largest pro bono legal network.
Legal Service for Independent Media Providing free legal services to support independent media.
