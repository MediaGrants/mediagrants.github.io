International newsletter Summer 2026
The 2026 Rousseeuw Prize for Statistics was awarded to the builders of R at the University Hall in Leuven. The Belgian science prize, worth $1 million, is regarded as the Nobel Prize of statistics. The international and independent jury, appointed by the King Baudouin Foundation, is honouring five members of the R Core Team for their exceptional contribution to statistical science. For almost three decades, they contributed on an unpaid basis to the development of R, the free software that powers the world of data. R has become indispensable wherever data are used, from clinical trials and central banks to data journalism.
Closing Event : Masterclass in Public Leadership
Join us on 9 November 2026 at the BELvue museum in Brussels for the Closing Event of the first edition of the Executive Masterclass in Public Leadership, organised by the King Baudouin Foundation and Vlerick Business School. The evening will bring together public-sector leaders, professionals and stakeholders to celebrate the participants completing the 2026 programme and to reflect on the future of public leadership in Belgium.

(International) Fundraising
Are you, as an organisation, working for the common good in Belgium and/or internationally, looking for effective ways of fundraising?
The King Baudouin Foundation has a broad network that can help you to raise funds among your supporters in Belgium, Europe, and/or internationally (e.g. in the USA, Canada, Asia, Australia, New Zealand etc.) and this in total transparency. Your project will gain greater visibility and your donors will benefit from tax advantages.

1. Look for a call for projects
2. Make an application
Once you have found a call that is in line with your project, you can make an application for support online.
On the call page, click on 'Submit your application’
Connect up to your account via https://www.mykbf.be/ , or create an account if this is your first application.
Complete the application form online (you can take your time and do it in several stages if you like).
When your application has been fully completed, send it in.
We will send you a confirmation by e-mail, as well as a pdf of your application.
For each call, a jury or management committee composed of independent experts evaluates the various projects submitted. We will inform you by e-mail to tell you whether your project has been accepted or not.

Are you looking for an effective means of fundraising in order to carry out one or more general interest projects?
Which solution to choose for fundraising?
The opening of a “Friends fund” makes it possible to collect donations in Belgium to finance specific projects.
Via Giving Europe and the Myriad alliance for borderless giving , an organisation based in Europe can fundraise from among donors based in Europe, the USA, Canada, Asia, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore etc.
Via a European Friends Fund , an organisation based outside the EU can fundraise among European donors.
Founder of the Audrey Jacobs Fund

International fundraising
Are you looking to raise funds for a charitable project? Would you welcome support from around the globe?
The Myriad alliance for borderless giving connects funders and project leaders, wherever they are, to make a better world, together. As a founding partner in this global network, the King Baudouin Foundation can help your organisation draw on the support of thousands of potential donors, notably in North America, Asia, and Australasia, and will package the most efficient legal, fiscal, and administrative structure to channel their support toward your goals, unhindered by frontiers.
Thanks to Myriad, charities in close to 100 countries can tap into support from donors (private individuals, businesses, and organisations) across four continents. By offering funders international legal and administrative services, as well as many of the tax advantages of domestic giving, Myriad helps grantees secure maximum benefit from the generosity of philanthropists overseas.
Myriad USA , draws on over two decades of experience in offering advice and made-to-measure solutions for US donors wishing to support good causes abroad, in the Asia-Pacific region, Europe, Africa, and Latin America. In addition to the fiscal facilities it permits, our partner is reputed for its in-depth knowledge of local legislation regarding international donations and the not-for-profit sector in Europe and Africa. Myriad USA also works with universities, museums, and other not-for-profit European and African institutions that wish to raise funds in the United States.
Myriad CANADA , supports philanthropists – private individuals, families, businesses and foundations – that wish to support good causes throughout the world. Founded in 2017, this philanthropic organisation registered in Canada develops personalised solutions that take account of each donor’s particular wishes. Thanks to Myriad CANADA, donors can fulfil their philanthropic ambitions via international projects conducted throughout the world and can benefit from Myriad CANADA’s knowledge of Canadian legislation and fiscal advantages.
