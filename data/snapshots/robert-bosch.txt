Calls for Proposals : A Transparent Path to Funding

We publish topic-specific calls for proposals to clearly indicate the areas where we are currently seeking new partnerships or projects—and where there is a realistic chance of funding. This approach offers several benefits for your organization:
Planning Ahead : Our calls show exactly which areas we are focusing on and which priorities matter most to us.
Better Chances of Success : You can invest your valuable resources in applications with realistic

--- relevant lines ---
 funding prospects.
Equal Opportunity : All organizations receive the same information and application options.
From Call to Funding Agreement
From the applications we receive, we select the most suitable project partners. These organizations are then invited to submit a full proposal—and we are happy to support you during this process if needed. The proposal summarizes objectives, target groups, and intended impact and forms the basis for funding approval. Once approved, we formalize the collaboration in a funding agreement, including grant amount, timeline, and financial plan.
As a partner organization, we aim to support you throughout every stage of the funding process.
Partnership-Based Funding
Effectiveness and sustainability are at the heart of our funding approach. Our close collaboration is built on trust and shared responsibility. Across our funding areas, we use a wide range of instruments: depending on the goal, we may lead initiatives ourselves, work bilaterally through partnerships and project funding, or join strong alliances.
Thank you for your interest in the work of the Robert Bosch Stiftung. As we award funding exclusively through our open calls, we are unfortunately unable to review or respond individually to unsolicited funding requests or project proposals. We invite you to visit our website regularly to learn about current funding opportunities and find calls that may be a good fit for your work.
There are currently no calls for proposals.
Our Funding Areas
Together with our partners, we focus on healthcare , education and global issues . We pursue this work through our own institutions, by supporting partner-led projects and by developing funding programs in Germany, Europe, Ukraine, West Africa, and West Asia.

Guidelines for the Proposal Phase

Step 1

Invitation by the foundation

Robert Bosch Stiftung specifically identifies potential recipient organizations and contacts them.
Duration : Individual
Please note : Unsolicited proposals are not accepted.

The proposal is created
You will prepare the detailed proposal with a comprehensive description and a finance plan.
Result : You send the proposal to Robert Bosch Stiftung.
The proposal is reviewed
Your proposal will be processed internally at the Robert Bosch Stiftung by the area of support team and the Grant Management team.
Decision : Grant contract or rejection.
For guidance on the project proposal (Step 2), you can optionally use the following template before entering your data in the portal:
• Project Proposal (docx)
Together with the proposal, you must fill out a finance plan in the portal and upload documents regarding the legal status of your organization.
After you have submitted your proposal, it will be reviewed by colleagues from the area of support and from Grant Management. You will have dedicated contact persons from both teams (for thematic and administrative matters), so that you can continue to work together with the same contacts in a spirit of trust beyond the proposal phase.
The review of your grant proposal may lead to further questions. The proposal can be revised if necessary. Particularly for proposals from organizations abroad and for first-time grants, we would like to establish contact with your administrative team and ask for additional information on the administrative processes within your organization. Based on a questionnaire, you will provide us with information about your standard processes (especially procurement and financial documentation processes) and submit your policies (e.g., travel policies). We call this exchange format ' Partner Consultation '. Please allow sufficient time to prepare and compile the documents.
Once all the necessary information has been reviewed and approved, you will receive a grant contract from us to be signed. We are happy to assist with any questions and to discuss the contents of the contract.
You can apply for a cost-neutral extension within the support period if you need more time for the project. In the Robert Bosch Stiftung portal , you just need to click on the "Extend support period" button and enter the new planned end date and a justification.
