A call for applications to ICORN will be announced early 2027
ICORN offers long-term, temporary residencies to writers, artists, and journalists who have faced or are facing persecution for exercising their right to freedom of expression through their profession.

The ICORN residency programme provides safe spaces for a standard period of two year, although some residencies are for one year. The purpose of the programme is to provide a safe space where writers, journalists, and artists can find safety while continuing their work and exercising their right to freedom of expression without fear of persecution, censorship, or interference.
ICORN will be issuing a call for applications early 2027. Please check in then for the official call and details on how to apply.
Any additional conditions beyond our regular requirements will be indicated when the call for applications is announced.

ICORN is not an urgent response organisation, and we cannot confirm if or when an eligible candidate for residency will receive an offer. We strongly encourage you to continue to search for alternative options to safety.
- It is free of charge to apply to ICORN: ICORN does not have application fees or other fees that are charged to those seeking an ICORN residency. ICORN does not have any representatives who are authorised to charge any fees to advise on the application process. If you are approached by people claiming to represent or act on behalf of ICORN, please inform us at application@icorn.org.
ICORN processes, stores, and protects personal data in accordance with Norwegian and European data protection laws. As part of your application, we store the information provided by you to help us understand your situation, verify the information provided, assess your eligibility, and identify suitable opportunities. This includes personal details, contact information, professional background, and documentation relating to your circumstances.
ICORN retains personal data for different time periods, depending on your application status and in accordance with ICORN s legal obligations. You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
