How to write a good project proposal

A good grant proposal starts with a clearly defined problem and an explanation of why it is important. You need to set out a specific objective and demonstrate what results you plan to achieve. The proposal should include a realistic action plan with defined stages and deadlines. It is important to justify the budget and explain what the funds will be spent on. It is also worth demonstrating the expected impact of the project and its benefits for the target audience.

The purpose of the contest

a) Engage civil society organizations from the most severely affected regions in Ukraine’s recovery planning processes, ensuring that recovery efforts address multiple dimensions, and support advocacy for sustainable development, human rights, non-discrimination, gender equality, and transparent governance in recovery strategies and territorial community development plans.

b) Provide opportunities for teams to strengthen their organizational capacity through the implementation of small grants, participation in a training programme, and partnership development.

Small Grants Competition
The Small Grants Competition supports local and regional civic initiatives that contribute to recovery, inclusion, gender equality and environmental sustainability in communities across the regions most affected by the war.
Organisations registered in Kyiv are also eligible to apply, provided that they work in communities in Kyiv region or in one of the other regions listed above.
Support for Grant Recipients
Grant recipients will take part in a mandatory capacity-building programme that includes:
Grant recipients will also be involved in regional centre networks that support local initiatives, facilitate learning and cooperation between communities and local authorities, as well as resilience forums and other development activities.
B. To provide opportunities for organisational development by supporting teams through the implementation of small grants, participation in the capacity-building programme and the development of partnerships.
Who Can Apply?
Organisations registered in territories of these regions that were under the control of the Government of Ukraine as of 24 February 2022 are eligible to apply. Organisations established during the full-scale war may also apply, provided that at least six months have passed since their initial registration.
An organisation does not have to be physically located at its registered address. Organisations that have relocated because of intense hostilities or occupation may apply after re-registering in government-controlled territory. However, a relocated applicant must have experience participating in civic initiatives related to the region where it was originally registered — for example, contributing to post-occupation recovery planning or remotely monitoring conditions in its home community.
