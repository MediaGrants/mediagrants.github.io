Today, we strengthen philanthropy through strategic giving, thematic events, research, campaigns, and empowering organizations in fundraising. We also present the VIRTUS
2. VIRTUS Award for Philanthropy
Since 2007, we have been presenting the VIRTUS Award to recognize and inspire companies, enterprises, and individuals whose philanthropic engagement contributes to the greater good in Serbia.
We collect data on citizens viewpoints and the perspectives of philanthropic participants regarding the culture of giving and motivation for participating in future actions. This helps shape giving promotion activities, supports non-profit organizations in developing funding strategies, and assists donors in recognizing the importance of the civil sector for societal development.
We’ve been supporting the development of community foundations in the region since 2011 .
At the beginning of 2019, we launched the program Our Local Foundation – The Community Decides! with the support of the Charles Stewart Mott Foundation. The program is currently being implemented in Serbia and Bosnia and Herzegovina, aiming to connect and support local foundations.
Community Foundations support initiatives, manage funds, mobilize communities, and raise resources while also providing non-financial support. In Serbia, they operate in Pančevo , Niš , and Stara Pazova , while in Bosnia and Herzegovina, they are present in Bijeljina, Zenica, and Sarajevo.
Through this program, we help civil society organizations enhance their fundraising skills by providing training, mentorship, and financial support.
The funds raised by these organizations are doubled by us, which increases their capacity to implement projects and ensure long-term sustainability.
With a focus on the long-term sustainability of organizations, we help them build strategic plans to diversify funding sources, develop capacities, and secure stable income, thereby reducing their reliance on external donations.
We are a member of the Council for Civil Society Development, which works to improve institutional and regulatory conditions for the free and sustainable operation of organizations. Civic Emergency Fund Activated: Support for Civil Society in the Region
The Civic Emergency Fund has officially been activated as a mechanism to provide rapid, flexible, and targeted support to civil society actors who are exposed to threats, pressure, or other forms of risk due to their work.
The Fund enables the rapid allocation of resources to respond to urgent security, legal, digital, and psychosocial challenges, as well as other forms of pressure and risk that may jeopardize the work of organizations and informal groups, the safety of activists, or the continuity of their activities.
The Fund exists to help organizations, groups, and activists continue operating when pressures become too great to manage on their own.
The Civic Emergency Fund is a joint initiative of the Trag Foundation and the Kosovo Civil Society Foundation (KCSF), established to provide support to civil society in the region.
More information about the Civic Emergency Fund and how to apply can be found here.
