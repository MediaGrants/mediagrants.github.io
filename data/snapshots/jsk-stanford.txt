JSK Journalism Fellowships at Stanford

Empowering and defending global innovators in journalism

Main content start

Based at Stanford University, the John S. Knight Journalism Fellowships empowers diverse journalism leaders to succeed as effective change agents, sustain democratic communities, and defend press freedom around the world.
Each year, we invite up to 20 journalists from around the world to join us for nine months at Stanford University. During this time, they work collaboratively to address journalism’s most urgent challenges. If you are a journalist with a passion for re-imagining and transforming the field, we encourag

The JSK Fellowships offers room to dig in, examine your assumptions and pursue a question that matters to you and to journalism. What would you explore? Apply for the next cohort.
The application is open for the next JSK cohort and we know you have a lot of questions. To help you, we have several webinars coming up where you can ask us anything. Sign up now to save your spot.
If you're ready to take a step back from your current role and dedicate yourself to this mission, our fellowship offers the support you need to develop leadership resilience. This includes personalized mentorship, specialized programming, and a collaborative environment designed to cultivate your skills and confidence.
We are committed to the many dimensions of diversity — professional and personal background, experience and thought — and work to ensure a welcoming and inclusive fellowship experience. We seek fellows who share our dedication to these ideals.
What happens after the fellowship year matters just as much as the year itself. The JSK Alumni Initiatives exist to keep former fellows connected to each other, plugged into new opportunities, and part of a network that understands the work.

People often ask: What do JSK Fellows actually do at Stanford?
The honest answer? There isn’t just one. Each fellowship year is an individual journey shaped by a fellow’s own questions, passions and goals.

--- relevant lines ---
At its heart, the JSK Fellowship is a year to reflect and reimagine what's possible, for journalism and for yourself.
Even the most accomplished journalists sometimes wonder, Do I really belong here? It's a natural question in a place that attracts people at the top of their fields. But one of the first lessons of the JSK Fellowship is that, yes, you do belong. Your voice, your experience and your curiosity are exactly why you're here.
Fellows' partners, whom we call affiliates, are welcome at most gatherings, which adds even more depth to the community. Cohorts often organize their own dinners, hikes and weekend adventures. The friendships and support system you build here can last well beyond the fellowship year.
Coaching also helps you slow down. It’s common for fellows to arrive at Stanford after years of nonstop deadlines and high-pressure work. Having a coach creates room to pause, take stock and ask what success really means to you, both professionally and personally.
