Call for Project and Event Proposals pertaining to the Defense of Human Rights for 2027
25.09.2026 - Artikel
The German Embassy Belgrade is happy to announce its call for Project and Event Proposals pertaining to the Defense of Human Rights for 2027. We welcome you to submit your project proposal until 23 October 2026 to pol-2@belg.diplo.de
Please be advised of the following eligibility criteria :
• Project Proposals should be formulated in English and include a budget overview
• Funding volume for projects: 50.000 €- 65.000 €
• Funding volume for Events: up to 10.000 €
• Project Starting date January 1st, 2027, Wrap Up by late November 2027
Thematic areas eligible for funding:
1995 – 2026
