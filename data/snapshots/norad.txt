Targeted Russian attacks have severely damaged Ukraine s energy supply. In 2026, Norway is providing Ukraine with a total of NOK 4.8 billion in support for electricity and heating.
Norad is the Norwegian Agency for Development Cooperation. We are responsible for managing large parts of Norway’s development assistance to other countries. We manage funding, provide expert advice and monitor results to help ensure that Norwegian development policy makes a real difference for people, the environment and the climate. Norad works on behalf of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Climate and Environment.

Grants Handbook for Civil Society and Other Private Actors
Published 7/30/2024
Published 7/30/2024
Updated 7/30/2024
Updated 7/30/2024
This section on Norad's webpages provides potential applicants for funding from Norad and existing grant recipients with an overview of the main expectations as well as legal and financial rules applicable to project/programme grants from Norad. These pages are relevant for civil society organisations and other private actors such as research institutes and private sector entities.
Note that the information on this webpage is not exhaustive and applicants are therefore asked to carefully review any documentation shared by Norad, specifically grant agreements, and direct communication with Norad staff. The grant agreement constitutes the legal basis for a grant. This text in these Guidelines is not legally binding.

Norad s Grant Scheme Rules
Published 9/6/2024
Published 9/6/2024
Updated 8/24/2025
Updated 8/24/2025
Grant scheme rules are a set of rules describing how the Norwegian Govenment's National Budget (Prop 1 S) should be operationalised in connection with allocation of funds to grant recipients. Calls for Proposals by Norad hence have to be aligned with the grant scheme rules for the relevant budget post. Norad is responsible for drafting and revising the grant scheme rules for budget posts that have been either fully or partially delegated from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to Norad. The Ministry approved them.
