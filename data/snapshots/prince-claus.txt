The CBF (Dutch Charities Regulator) grants a seal of approval to organizations that meet strict quality standards. This recognition signifies that the charity adheres to high standards of accountability and effectiveness. While requirements vary between larger and smaller organizations, about 80 percent of private donations are made to charities under CBF supervision. Donating to a CBF-approved charity ensures that your contributions are used responsibly, the organization is transparent, and it undergoes independent evaluations. Read more about the charitable status of the Prince Claus Fund here .
Donations are received by our Fundraising department and assessed according to our ethical donation policy. Each donation undergoes an investigation, including donor background and donation history, with more extensive checks for larger donations.
Donations must align with our mission, values, and legal standards. Legal counsel may be consulted for certain types of gifts. Donations that conflict with our mission, pose reputational risks, or have legal or ethical concerns which jeopardise the independence of the Fund may be rejected. Reasons for refusal are communicated clearly to donors when appropriate.

American Friends of the Prince Claus Fund may claim the maximum tax benefits allowed by U.S. tax law for each donation. This is made possible by donating through Myriad USA , which is a public charity operating under 501(c)(3) of the IRC US.
The Prince Claus Fund has received its Equivalency Determination (ED) from NGOsource, confirming that we are equivalent to a U.S. public charity. Individual donors can support our mission to amplify the transformative power of culture, with confidence that their contributions meet U.S. tax-deductibility standards.

The Prince Claus Fund is registered with the Dutch Tax Administration as an Algemeen Nut Beogende Instelling (ABNI), or a cultural Public Benefit Organization (PBO). This designation allows all donations to the Fund to be tax-deductible in the Netherlands, with additional advantages for long-term contributions. As a private individual, your donation can be deducted at 125% of the amount donated from your taxable income. Additionally, you can combine your donations to multiple cultural organizations for these benefits. For more information, visit the Belastingdienst website .
Making periodic donations offers the maximum tax advantage. This involves contributing at least €100 per year for a minimum of five years. To determine your individual tax deductibility, please check the relevant guidelines.
