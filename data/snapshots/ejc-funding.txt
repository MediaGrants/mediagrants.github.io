A Named Fund
Would you like to support journalism for a longer period and in an impactful way? This can be done by setting up a named fund.
You deposit the desired amount in a fund whose name you choose. If you make this as a periodic donation (in the Netherlands), your contributions will be tax-deductible. Donations through your named fund are mentioned in the annual report, the annual accounts, and our website.
