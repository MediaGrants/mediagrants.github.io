International Partners Call Organisational Development (OD)

Adapting organisational governance, processes and practices to build equitable partnerships.

We welcome applications from international organisations, networks, and coalitions that have peacebuilding at the core of their mission and a track record in enabling locally-led peacebuilding, preferably in the regions we focus on. The deadline for applications is 17 November 2025.

What is this Call about?

International peacebuilding organisations operate in increasingly challenging contexts; the space and funding for civil society action is shrinking while norms underpinning accountable and participatory pea

--- relevant lines ---
cebuilding are being eroded. Despite increasingly divided and hostile contexts, most international organisations remain engaged and committed to inclusive peacebuilding. However, the imperative for adaptation is strong. Often in dialogue with partners, international organisations are rethinking and adapting their roles, strategies, and structures to build more resilient local organisations, networks, and societies with strong links to international policy fora and funding.
With this Call, we aim to support the courageous intermediary organisations that are not only adapting to new political and financial realities but also seizing this moment of reckoning to further transform their roles, governance, funding and partnership practices to make good on the long-standing promise of localisation. Now, more than ever, it is time to ensure those most affected by conflict can lead the way in building sustainable peace – enabled by mutually accountable, equitable partnerships.
Selected applicants will receive our process accompaniment support and a grant of up to 40,000 CHF per phase to cover external expertise and process costs (for example, for meetings, travel, and communication). The duration of a support phase is approximately a year; the change process may unfold over up to 3 phases, depending on needs and progress.
Organisations can apply for one of two types of support:
Organisational development support to ensure a strategic and operational adaptation to current political and funding realities while strengthening their commitment to locally-led peacebuilding. See our organisational development support process here .
