We fund local legal centres to provide free legal support to journalists in their country.

We provide grants and technical support to organisations around the world to enable them to provide free, high quality legal support directly to journalists and media outlets in their countries.

Last year we funded 21 organisations in 19 countries.
Our aim is to expand this network, creating better local-level access to legal support for independent media who lack the financial means to access it at commercial rates.

If you face legal threats because of your journalism, we can help.

We support journalists – including citizen journalists, bloggers, broadcasters, photojournalists, cartoonists or fact-checkers – and news outlets when they are confronted with legal action as a result of their reporting.
We can provide funding for your legal defence and we can help you find a lawyer. Our legal team can also provide technical legal support to your lawyer.
We can also help you bring legal action to compel the state to protect your rights if they have been violated, including to investigate abuses and bring perpetrators of attacks to justice.

We fund local legal centres to provide free legal support to journalists in their country.

We combine financial assistance with practical support to organisations that either have, or wish to establish, a legal aid unit to provide free, high-quality legal support to journalists, citizen journalists, and media outlets facing legal threats because of their reporting.

See all of our funded partners

Last year we funded 18 organisations in 17 countries.
Our aim is to expand this network, creating better local-level access to legal support for independent media who lack the financial means to access it at commercial rates.
