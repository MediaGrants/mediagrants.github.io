التقدم بطلب للحصول على منحة

عملية التقدُّم لطلب المنح

تُدعى المبادرات والمنظمات العاملة في مجال العدالة بين الجنسين إلى النظر في عملية التقدم الواردة أدناه

التأكد من أن مجموعتكن مؤهلة

الاطلاع على متطلباتنا ومعاييرنا بغية التأكد من أن مجموعتكن مناسبة تماما للمنح التي نقدمها.

إنشاء ملف تعريف وتقديمه

تسجيل منظمتكن عن طريق تقديم ملف التعريف المؤسسي الخاص بالمنظمة. وسيكون ملف التعريف الخاص محفوظا على نحو يضمن أمانه وسريته.

وسوف نتصل بكن إذا وجدنا منحة لمجموعتكن*

إذا كانت هناك منحة يمكن أن تستفيد منها مجموعتكن، سوف نتصل بكن وندعوكن لتقديم طلب.

*برجاء ملاحظة أننا لن نتمكن من الرد على جميع الجهات المتقدمة، ولكن سيظل ملف التعريف المؤسسي الخاص بكن محفوظا في نظامنا لمدة عامين، وسيُنظر فيه لتحديد مدى ملاءمته لجميع المنح المفتوحة والمستقبلية.

معايير المنح

ينبغي أن تستوفي مجموعتكن المعايير التالية:

أن تكون خارج الولايات المتحدة
بوجه عام، لا نموِّل المنظمات التي تتخذ من الولايات المتحدة مقرا لها، مع بعض الاستثناءات

Our Approach and Impact

Funding that trusts feminist organizations to lead

Real progress toward gender justice starts with grassroots feminist organizations. Rooted in their communities, they understand local needs and hold the knowledge, trust, and lived experience to create lasting change.

That's why we provide flexible, long-term support, so they can set their own priorities and decide what's next.

Who We Fund

We fund grassroots feminist organizations around the world, particularly those working in countries where organizing is dangerous or restricted, and flexible support is harder to access.

They are the first to recognize emerging threats and solutions, and the first to respond when a crisis hits. But too often, they're the last to receive funding.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is gender justice?

For us, gender justice means centering the leadership, lived experiences, and diverse needs of women, girls, trans, and gender non-conforming people. It requires advancing equality by addressing the structural barriers that prevent people from fully exercising their rights and achieving equal opportunities, access, and outcomes.

We know that no two people face the same barriers. The fight for women’s rights is deeply connected to racial justice, queer justice, immigration justice, climate justice, and many other movements for justice.

Who does Global Fund for Women fund?
We are a global intermediary feminist fund. We raise money and grant it directly to grassroots feminist organizations and activists around the world, rather than running programs ourselves.
Our partners are crisis responders. They are abortion access networks where laws are changing. They are the lawyers, shelter leaders, and organizers working beyond the reach of most institutional funding. They are grassroots groups taking action, organizing protests, and challenging laws in over 180 countries, often at great personal risk.
We fund grassroots feminist organizations across Latin America and the Caribbean, Asia and the Pacific, Africa, Southwest Asia and North Africa, and Eastern Europe. We support organizations of all sizes and legal structures, including groups that operate outside traditional nonprofit models when that best serves their communities and context. Read more about their stories here .
How does Global Fund for Women identify new partners?
Does Global Fund for Women have open calls or accept unsolicited grant applications?
No. We don’t accept unsolicited funding applications.
In the past, we received thousands of funding requests each year but could only support a small percentage of them. We also recognized that grassroots organizations were spending significant time and resources preparing applications without knowing whether funding was available. Instead, we find partners through trusted relationships with grassroots leaders, advisors, and movement networks, combined with publicly available data that help us spot emerging needs.
If you're looking for funding opportunities, we've compiled a list of organizations that support women's rights and gender justice around the world.
What does flexible funding mean?
It means our partners decide how to use their grants based on their communities' needs — not ours. We give flexible, multi-year funding with minimal reporting requirements, so our partners can focus on the transformational work they already do and use resources where they are needed most.
