Welcome to the Open Information Partnership. We are a diverse network of organisations and individuals united in our determination to expose and counter disinformation.

We are investigative journalists, charities, think tanks, academics, NGOs, activists, and factcheckers, active in over 24 countries.
Since February 2019 we have been standing firm against the rising tide of disinformation – in the news, on social media and across our public discourse – which we believe to be an existential threat to democracy.
The Open Information Partnership allows us to share skills, knowledge and resources to help us do what we already do – but more of it, and better.
By working together, we can improve our understanding of the threat posed by disinformation; we can expose lies and false narratives, promote policy and research-led solutions, and build a healthy, secure and resilient media space.
Democracy cannot thrive without honest, accurate and freely available information about the world around us.
To find out more, please get in touch.
