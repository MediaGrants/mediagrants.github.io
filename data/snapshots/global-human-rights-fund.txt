Migrants’ Rights
Our Strategic Outlook, 2025–2030
Over the past decade, civic space for human rights activism has become contested or closed across much of the world. In this challenging context, our work resourcing frontline human rights movements is more critical than ever. Our 2025–2030 Strategic Outlook consolidates what we have learned over more than 20 years of work, and sets out our five-year strategic focus and priorities for the road ahead.

Migrants Rights

We believe in the right to move freely.

But as global conflict and the climate crisis displace millions around the world, people on the move—including migrants, asylum-seekers, and refugees—face militarized borders, violent nationalism, and state-sanctioned persecution.

How We Support Migrants Rights
Promoting Migrant-Led Organizations
Migrant communities should be able to advocate on their own behalf. We help uplift migrant-led initiatives and empower them to be active leaders in civil society.
Protecting Migrants’ Rights Defenders
Migrant activists and their allies face xenophobic threats, intimidation, and attacks. We make sure they have the resources, networks, and support they need to stay safe.

Global Activist Alliance

The Global Activist Alliance comprises dedicated donors who provide vital support for grassroots activists. Our members are key to making the long-term investments needed for activists to grow their movements and make lasting change.

Our Giving Levels

Membership offers special benefits, honoring your extraordinary commi

Donate over $250
With a donation of $250 and above, you will help marginalized communities stand up for their rights. In recognition of your support, you will receive a branded gift, updates on activists, and our annual report.
Donate $250
Donate over $500
By making a gift of $500 or more, you will help activists make critical gains toward equality. As a thank you, you’ll receive a branded gift, recognition on our virtual giving wall, and our annual report—highlighting the difference you’re making for activists.
Donate $500
Donate over $1,000
As a Trailblazer, you help power grassroots movements for justice across communities, issues, and generations. With a gift of $1,000 or more, you’ll be recognized with a branded gift, featured on our virtual giving wall and in our annual report, and receive personal updates on the impact you’re making around the world.
Donate $1,000
Donate over $2,500
With a gift of $2,500 or more, you become a Champion—making it possible for human rights defenders to safeguard democracy and their communities. You’ll be thanked virtually and in our annual report, receive a branded gift, get exclusive insights through our Fund in Focus newsletter, and be invited to special FGHR events.
Donate over $5,000
Change makers provide transformational support for grassroots activists tackling critical challenges, beginning with gifts of $5,000. In addition to recognition virtually and in our annual report, you’ll receive a branded gift , special insights through our Fund in Focus newsletter, and exclusive invitations to connect with activists and FGHR leadership.
Investing in the Fund is money well spent! The Fund is so well connected. The groups they support know what they’re doing. And it’s powerful to be a part of making long-term change possible.

Give Through a Donor-Advised Fund
Donor-advised funds, or DAFs, are charitable giving programs that allow you to receive immediate tax benefits while supporting your favorite causes.
Use DAF Direct to make a contribution to the Fund for Global Human Rights from your existing donor-advised fund or talk with your financial advisor about setting up a DAF and including us in your charitable giving.

Donor-Advised Grants
We accept donor-advised grants (DAG) of $10,000 or more.
If you are interested in establishing a DAG with the Fund for Global Human Rights, or other opportunities to make a meaningful contribution to the courageous work of activists, please reach out to Anna Keye, senior manager of individual giving, at anna@globalhumanrights.org .
